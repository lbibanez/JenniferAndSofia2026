Our Journey

THE STORY OF US

We met in June 2021 during a biodiversity fieldwork in Pagudpud, Ilocos Norte. At first, we were simply two people sharing the same place — the same trails, long days, and quiet evenings.
We were just colleagues, exchanging brief, practical conversations and noticing each other only in passing. Then plans shifted. Because of a flight mix-up, Sofia stayed five more days. It felt as if life itself was asking for a little more time. With the work nearly done, the days softened. We found ourselves talking through meals and lingering long after everyone else had gone to rest.
Somewhere in those unhurried evenings, a quiet understanding found us — that a person so far from home could begin to feel like home, and that someone we had only just met could feel gently, unmistakably familiar. By the time those five days ended, goodbye felt unfamiliar, as though we were interrupting something still in the middle of being said. So we kept talking.

In December 2021, we became a couple, making space for each other across the distance between Ilocos and Davao through late-night calls, shared routines, and the quiet comfort of knowing someone was there. In January 2023, we returned to Pagudpud. At Little Batanes, where it all began, a question was asked. Will you marry me? Yes.

By March 2025, we were finally living in the same place in Davao, where simple routines slowly became a life — steady and ours. Getting married isn’t the start of our story. It is simply continuing the life that began with five unexpected days — the days that made us home to each other.
